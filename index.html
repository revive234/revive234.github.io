Jian Yu

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student at Nanjing University of Science and Technology (NJUST), advised by Prof. Jinhui Tang. I am also working closely with Dr. Fei Shen.

My research interests include visual generation, latent knowledge in large models, and AI safety. I am particularly interested in understanding the internal mechanisms of AI models and leveraging their latent knowledge to improve the controllability, reliability, and safety of generative and multimodal AI systems.
